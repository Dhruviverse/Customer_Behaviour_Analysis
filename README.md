# **Customer Behavior Dashboard**

## **Project Overview**

An interactive **Power BI Customer Behavior Dashboard** developed to analyze customer purchasing patterns, sales performance, and revenue trends. The project combines **Excel/CSV, Python, SQL, and Power BI** to transform raw data into meaningful business insights.

## **Key Insights**

* **3.9K+ Customers** analyzed
* **Average Purchase Amount:** $59.76
* **Average Review Rating:** 3.75
* Customer distribution based on **Subscription Status**
* **Revenue and Sales by Category**
* **Revenue and Sales by Age Group**
* Analysis of **Gender, Shipping Type, and Product Category**
* Interactive **slicers** for dynamic data exploration

## **Tools & Technologies**

**Python** – Data cleaning and preprocessing
**SQL** – Data analysis and querying
**Excel/CSV** – Data source and data preparation
**Power BI** – Data visualization and interactive dashboard

## **Dashboard Features**

The dashboard provides an easy-to-use interface with interactive filters for **Subscription Status, Gender, Category, and Shipping Type**, allowing users to explore customer behavior and identify important sales and revenue trends.

## **Objective**

The main objective of this project is to convert raw customer data into an **interactive business intelligence dashboard** that helps understand customer behavior, evaluate sales performance, and support **data-driven decision-making**.
